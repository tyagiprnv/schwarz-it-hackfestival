# Schwarz IT Hackathon - Carbon Footprint Reduction 🌍🚀

## Overview 🌟
Developed during the **Schwarz IT Hackathon (September 2024)**, this project aims to slash carbon emissions through smart inventory optimization. Tailored for retail giants like Lidl (part of Schwarz Gruppe), it was crafted by a team featuring Ayush Mukherjee, a Software Developer with chops from Bosch and Indus Net Technologies. Powered by full-stack skills (React.js, Node.js), cloud tech (Docker, Azure), and data-driven magic, this solution cuts waste, boosts sustainability, and vibes with Schwarz IT’s mission to level up business processes with cutting-edge IT. 🌱💻

## Features 🎯
- **Smart Inventory Management** 📊: Crunches historical sales and food expiry data to optimize stock, slashing overstock and waste.
- **Real-Time Bidding System** 💰: Sells near-expiry goodies at discounts, tackling food waste like a boss.
- **Green Points System** 🌳: Rewards eco-friendly buys with points you can cash in to plant trees in German forests—your pick!
- **Targeted Marketing** 📣: Harnesses Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for personalized, green-focused ads that spark engagement.

## The Vision 🌈
This solution fights decarbonization by predicting sales trends and mastering inventory with flair:  
- **Trend Analysis** 🔍: Spots fast-sellers and shelf-sitters, factoring in shelf-life, and sorts them into perishable or non-perishable squads.  
- **Customer Insights** 🧑‍🤝‍🧑: Dives into a self-made database of member customers to map buying habits, splitting small biz owners from retail shoppers.  
- **Bidding for Bulk Buyers** 🏪: Rolls out a bidding system for near-expiry items—perfect for restaurants or farms that gobble goods fast—cutting waste and boosting small-scale hustlers.  
- **Profit & Sustainability** 💡: Ups profitability by ditching expired stock, offers sweet deals for small biz, and nudges eco-vibes forward.  
- **Green Points Incentive** 🌲: Hooks buyers of near-expiry items with points funding tree planting via NGO tie-ups, keeping it real and transparent.  
- **User Empowerment** ✊: Lets customers choose their fave German forest for tree planting, building trust and connection.  

We hope this idea lights you up—blending tech, sustainability, and a sprinkle of good vibes! ✨🌿
